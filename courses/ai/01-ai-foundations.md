# AI Foundations

Artificial intelligence is the practice of building systems that can perform tasks that normally require human judgment, perception, language understanding, reasoning, or adaptation. In software terms, AI is not one single tool. It is a family of techniques that help applications classify, predict, recommend, search, generate, and automate.

## What AI Can Do

AI is useful when the rules are difficult to write manually but examples, patterns, or feedback are available.

- Classification: assign an item to a category, such as spam or not spam.
- Prediction: estimate a future value, such as demand, churn, or delivery time.
- Recommendation: rank items based on user behavior or similarity.
- Search: find relevant information from large collections.
- Generation: create text, code, images, summaries, plans, or structured data.
- Automation: combine reasoning with tools to complete a workflow.

## AI, ML, Deep Learning, and Generative AI

AI is the broad field. Machine learning is a major part of AI where models learn from data instead of being manually programmed with every rule. Deep learning is a machine learning approach based on neural networks with many layers. Generative AI is a category of AI that produces new content such as text, code, images, audio, or video.

## A Simple AI System

A practical AI system usually has these parts:

1. A user problem that is worth solving.
2. Data or context that helps the system make decisions.
3. A model or workflow that transforms input into output.
4. Evaluation to check whether the output is useful.
5. Product design that makes the AI safe and understandable for users.

## Key Terms

- Model: a learned or configured system that maps inputs to outputs.
- Training: the process of learning from examples.
- Inference: using a trained model to produce an answer.
- Feature: an input signal used by a model.
- Label: the correct answer used during supervised learning.
- Prompt: instructions and context given to a generative model.
- Token: a small unit of text processed by language models.

## Learning Goal

The goal of this course is not just to explain AI concepts. The goal is to help learners build practical AI products with clear problem framing, good data habits, strong evaluation, and responsible deployment.
